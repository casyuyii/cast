After a period of not much progress an incredibly powerful idea emerged to lift the search for machine intelligence out of its rut. Why not try to build artificial brains by copying how real biological brains worked?

非常大胆的想法.

Attention turned to the architectural differences. Traditional computers processed data very much sequentially, and in pretty exact concrete terms. There is no fuzziness or ambiguity about their cold hard calculations. Animal brains, on the other hand, although apparently running at much slower rhythms, seemed to process signals in parallel, and fuzziness was a feature of their computation.

比较核心的在于结尾: fuzziness was a feature of their computation. 模糊不清是他们计算的一种特性. 

这是可以深省的一段话. 

我们会有假设, 会有直觉, 会有快思考. 当得出结论时, 实际我们并不是十分清楚/了解并可以用语言解释自己的行为(就像这次和设计师沟通的过程, 我们需要知道这种布局为什么好看, 但是设计师实际自己可能也并不是很能清楚地解释自己的行为, 只是觉得这样好看, 而长年的经验让他们这样的直觉是正确的) 计算机则需要的是全方位了解这些信息, 因为计算机本质只能做计算, 让他计算的东西, 必须是十分清楚定义的.
